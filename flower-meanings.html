Historical Heritage

The Victorian Art of Silent Floriography

During the 19th century, strict etiquette forbade open confessions of emotion. Lovers and friends turned to floriography—the language of flowers—to send secret, coded bouquets known as "tussie-mussies."

A single blush rose spoke of unspoken passion, while white lilies pledged pure devotion and sunflowers radiated faithful adoration. At Floral Delight, we honor this timeless heritage in every bouquet we handcraft.
